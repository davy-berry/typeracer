Typing Test Instructions

Welcome to Type Racer! Follow these steps to complete the typing test:

  • Select a difficulty level from the dropdown. This determines the sample sentence you will type.
  • Start typing in the text area. The test begins automatically when you type the first character.
  • Words typed correctly will be highlighted in blue and incorrect words will be highlighted in red.
  • When you have finished typing, press the Enter key to stop the test.
  • The results panel will display your difficulty level, completion time, and WPM score.
  • Click Retry to load a new sentence at the same difficulty and try again.

Tips for a better performance:

  • Keep your hands on the keyboard home row and avoid looking at the keyboard.
  • Focus on accuracy first, then increase your speed.
  • Take deep breaths and keep your posture comfortable.
